## 2. THUMBNAILS

Three ready designs are in this folder (1280×720, the exact YouTube spec):

- `thumbnail_B_scale.svg` — giant Sun, tiny Earth + **"YOU ARE THIS SMALL"**
- `thumbnail_C_astronaut.svg` — lone astronaut + **"DON'T TAKE OFF YOUR HELMET"**

**How to export to PNG:** open the `.svg` in Chrome → right-click → "Save as" won't rasterize, so instead use any free "SVG to PNG 1280x720" converter, OR open in Figma/Canva/Photopea and export PNG. Once your Seedance renders exist, you can also drop a real video still **behind** the text layer for max realism.

**Thumbnail best practices baked in:** one focal subject, 3–4 huge words, heavy text stroke for readability at small size, high contrast, face-free (fits faceless brand), readable on a phone.

**Recommended pick:** **Thumbnail A (black hole)** — highest mystery + the cleanest silhouette at small size.
